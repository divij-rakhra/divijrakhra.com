The SEO experiment: ranking Bricx #1 for "best UX agencies"

July 2026 · 3 min

The goal: dominate the "best ux agencies" keyword SERP for bricxlabs.com.

The hypothesis

We will use a mix of four angles:

EMD directories. Buying "topuxdesignagencies.com" as a keyword-match domain and getting backlinks for it.

Using LinkedIn and Medium articles for parasite SEO.

Our own SEO blog, internally linked heavily.

Buying spots, or exchanging them with other agencies and directories.

The process

EMD strategy

Buy a domain, build a directory, get some backlinks, and push it to DR 10. Start ranking on the pillar page, "top ux agencies."

LinkedIn parasite

From Siddharth's account, create a listicle of the best UX agencies based on client experiences, or some real-world data. Get backlinks to it as well.

Medium parasite

Ask an established Medium creator to write a post about "top ux agencies," then get backlinks to it.

SEO blog

Revive the existing written blog on top UX design agencies and get backlinks to its dedicated page.

Buying spots

Find link-exchange opportunities with the players on the first page of the SERP.
