How we got 3,000+ signups for our AI SaaS in 24 hours

AimPut a new AI product in front of designers with no ads, no audience and no influencers.

ApparatusFray, our Figma plugin. One LinkedIn launch post, a launch video and a comment to DM automation.

Procedure

  1. Position it in one line people already get
  2. Write the post so people want to comment
  3. DM the link to everyone who comments

Result3,000+ signups in 24 hours, $0 on ads

ConclusionContent that rewards engagement spreads itself. The tactic gets weaker every year. The principle doesn't.

Expt. No.2DateSep 2026TopicAI search
The Zero-DR Citation: how two brand-new domains started steering ChatGPT's recommendations

AimFind out if a brand-new domain with zero authority can get cited inside ChatGPT answers.

ApparatusTwo exact-match domains, a ranked directory built with Claude Code in an afternoon, Peec AI.

Procedure

  1. Buy the domain that is the search query
  2. Publish one ranked list with its method
  3. Do zero outreach and watch the answers

Result113 ChatGPT citations in 8 days, from 3 backlinks

ConclusionAll good SEO is good AEO, but not all good AEO is good SEO.

Expt. No.3DateNov 2026TopicAI search
Top 5 beats top 1: what your position on a list does to your AI mentions

AimCheck if a higher spot on a paid list gets you named more often by AI.

Apparatus14 lists AI engines read, 25 buyer prompts, Peec AI, three $500 a month spots.

Procedure

  1. Measure the mention rate at every position
  2. Buy #5 on three lists, not top 3 at $1,500
  3. Compare the same prompts before and during

Result#1 and #5 get named equally often

ConclusionPay to be inside the cut, never for #1.

Expt. No.4DateNov 2026TopicAgents
The agent office: how 42 agents run my marketing

AimHand every repetitive marketing job to an agent and keep only judgement for humans.

ApparatusOne codebase, an hourly clock, a verified facts file, Slack and one app per department.

Procedure

  1. Give every agent one job and a Never list
  2. Make agents review agents every week
  3. Let safe changes ship without approval

Result42 agents, 0 failing, $21 of AI in 30 days

ConclusionHumans own the outcome. Agents execute and scale the strategy we agree on together.
